# Drosophila pseudoobscura project

This repository contains the scripts and notebooks used for
the analyses presented in ‘Differential interactions of heteromorphic sperm classes with spermatheca underlie the cryptic female selection in Drosophila pseudoobscura’.

## Analysis workflow

00. Replot published data (.ipynb file)
01. PCA (.Rmd & .html file)
02. Global correlation (.Rmd & .html file)
03. Differential protein expression (.Rmd & .html file)
04. Differential gene expression (.Rmd & .html file)
05. Tissue-specific genes & housekeeping tau (.Rmd & .html file)
06. Heatmap (.Rmd & .html file)
07. Chromosomal distribution of sperm (.Rmd & .html file)
08. Protein and gene number changes (.ipynb file) + Venn proportion change (.Rmd & .html file)
09. Evolutionary rate covariation (.Rmd & .html file)
10. dN/dS calculation (.ipynb file) 
11. LFQ and RPKM changes (.ipynb file) 
12. Group bubble plots (.Rmd & .html file)
